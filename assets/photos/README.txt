The gallery currently loads its 6 photos from Unsplash (external URLs) and
the hero section has no background photo — both by original design.

To switch the gallery to local files instead:
1. Save your own photos here as photo1.jpg ... photo6.jpg (and hero.jpg if
   you add one to the hero section).
2. In index.html, replace each <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/..."
   with src="assets/photos/photoN.jpg".
