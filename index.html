Chapter 02 · Memory gallery

Not old photographs — new light

Chapter 03 · Opening envelope

A letter, sealed in wax

Tap the seal. Some words are meant to be unfolded.

My Little Cat

It has only been a few weeks.All nights I fell asleep thinking of your name.

I still remember the first message — how something so small could feel like a door opening. I did not know then that I would start looking for you the way people look for sunrise.

You love cats. I love the way your voice changes when you talk about them. I love learning you slowly, carefully, like a poem I refuse to rush.

This is not a museum of old memories. We have not stacked years yet. What we have is better: a beginning that already feels like home.

I built you this little sky. Walk through it. Few weeks down. Forever to go — if you will have me.
Always yours
♥

Chapter 04 · A poem

Read this slowly

Lines arrive the way we did — one, then another, then all at once.

I have known you for a few weeks

and already the moon looks different.

You love small creatures

with quiet paws and enormous hearts —

I think that is how you love, too.

Soft. Sudden. Entire.

If I could, I would fold this month

into a sunlit windowsill

and let you nap inside it.

Stay. Stay. Stay.

The story is only on page one.

Chapter 05 · Our love timeline

Few weeks of weather

From the first text to tonight. A booming little history.

Day 01

The first hii

A message. A spark. The beginning we did not know was a beginning. I read it twice, then a third time, smiling like a fool.

Day 05

We could not stop talking

Hours disappeared between us. I did not mind. The ordinary room I was sitting in felt newly invented.

day 7

I learned your laugh

And now I collect it like gold. If the month had a soundtrack, it would be that sound, over and over.

day 10

You and your cats

You showed me your world — the soft one, the playful one, the one with whiskers. I wanted to live in it.

Tonight

A little website, a big heart

I made you a sky because flowers wilt and texts scroll away. This stays. Scroll it whenever you need to remember.

Tonight

Still just the beginning

Few weeks old and already fully in love. I am not asking the future to hurry. I am asking it to keep looking like you.
